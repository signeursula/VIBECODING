## Monster High Solitaire
I really loved the Monster High dolls when I was the age 8 to 13 (actually, I still love them), therefore I wanted to make my own Monster High themed set of 52 playing cards. Even though AI probably could have made the card designs for me, I enjoyed making the graphics myself, and then Gemini could program the code for me.
