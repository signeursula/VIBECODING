## Den super nemme kogebog
This is my "super easy cookbook", because I am a lazy girl, and I hate cooking. So I have made my easiest dishes into a PDF, which I through GitHub easily can find on my phone. The recipes can be made with things I typically already have in my fridge or cabinet, like canned or frozen things. All of the ingredients can be bought in my beloved Rema1000.
