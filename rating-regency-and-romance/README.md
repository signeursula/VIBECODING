## Rating Regency & Romance movies and series
The last year I have been totally obsessed with late 1800s to early 1900s period dramas. Everything from Bridgerton to Enola Holmes. I just love love love a good ball, some beautiful gowns and some romantic drama! Therefore I wanted to vibecode a website, where I could give the series and movies a rating, and have a cute way to keep track of everything I have watched.
