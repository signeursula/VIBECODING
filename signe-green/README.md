## Gæt en Signe-grøn
This was a very easy-made little project I made. It's a game that hopefully will help my friends and familt learn which green I refer to, when I say that I love the color green. I love love love the right shade of green (read: "Signe-green"), but I hate the opposite. And because my family get confused when buying me gifts, I wanted to make a game, so they'll learn which shades of green I actually like! Enjoy.
